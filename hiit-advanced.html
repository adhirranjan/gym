Advanced · Bodyweight + Plyometric

Advanced HIIT Program

The beginner plan, progressed — harder variations, longer work, more rounds, shorter rest, real finishers.

Advanced Protocol

  • Interval: 40–45 sec work / 15 sec rest (was 20s / rest as needed)
  • Rounds: 4–5 per circuit (was 2–3) · 60–90 sec between rounds
  • Intensity: RPE 8–9 — near-max on work intervals, controlled on strength moves
  • Session: warm-up 8–10 min → work 30–40 min → finisher → cool-down 5–7 min
  • Overload: each week add a round, cut 5 sec rest, or add tempo/load. Deload every 4th week (drop to 3 rounds).
