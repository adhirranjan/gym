Fat Loss & Lean Muscle · Workout Plan

Schedule #2

Weekly system — lean + fat loss, moderate weight, higher reps, short rest.

🧠 Workout Weekly System

Mon → Upper (Lean + Strength mix)

Tue → Legs (Tone + Burn)

Wed → HIIT + Core

Thu → Full Body Burn

Fri → Plyo + Explosive

Sat → CrossFit Fat Burn

Monday (Lean Body Focus – Fat Loss + Toning)

👉 Focus: Higher calorie burn + muscle toning (not heavy hypertrophy)

🔥 1. Warm-up

  • Jump Rope / Jumping Jacks – 3 min
  • Arm Circles – 1 min each
  • Dynamic stretch – 2 min

🔄 2. Fat Loss Circuit (Full Upper + Core Hybrid)

👉 4 rounds (less rest = more fat burn)

  • Incline Push-ups – 15 reps
  • Resistance Band Rows / Light DB Rows – 15 reps
  • Arnold Press (light weight) – 12 reps
  • TRX Rows / Inverted Rows – 10–12 reps
  • Plank Shoulder Tap – 20 reps

⏱ Rest: 30–40 sec only

🔥 3. Functional + Core Burn Circuit

👉 3 rounds

  • Battle Ropes / Fast Punches – 30 sec
  • Medicine Ball Slams / Dumbbell Slams – 12 reps
  • Mountain Climbers – 40 sec
  • Russian Twists – 20 reps
  • Hanging Knee Raises – 12 reps

⚡ 4. Cardio Finisher

Option A (Best for fat loss)

  • Treadmill incline walk
  • 10–15 min
  • Speed: moderate
  • Incline: 8–12

Option B (HIIT)

  • Sprint 20 sec + walk 40 sec × 10 rounds

🧘 5. Cool Down

  • Chest + shoulder + core stretch

Tuesday (Lean Legs + Fat Loss Focus)

🔥 1. Warm-Up (same structure, slightly more dynamic)

  • High Knees – 2 min
  • Leg Swings – 1 min each
  • Walking Lunges – 2 min
  • Bodyweight Squats – 20 reps

🔄 2. Lower Body Fat-Burn Circuit

👉 4 rounds (short rest = key)

  • Bodyweight Squats (slow + controlled) – 20 reps
  • Walking Lunges – 15 each leg
  • Glute Bridges – 20 reps
  • Step-Ups (bench) – 12 each leg
  • Standing Calf Raises – 20 reps

⏱ Rest: 30–40 sec

⚡ 3. Functional Conditioning (Game Changer)

👉 3 rounds

  • Kettlebell Swings (light/moderate) – 15 reps
  • Jump Squats – 12 reps
  • Speed Skaters – 30 sec
  • Wall Sit – 40 sec

🔥 4. Cardio Finisher (VERY IMPORTANT)

👉 Choose 1:

Option A (Best for fat loss)

  • Stair Climber / Steps
  • 10–15 min continuous

Option B (HIIT)

  • Cycle Sprint:
  • 20 sec fast + 40 sec slow × 10 rounds

🧘 5. Cool Down

  • Hamstrings
  • Quads
  • Glutes
  • Hip flexors

Wednesday (HIIT + Core Shred Day)

🔥 1. Warm-Up

  • Skipping / Jump Rope – 3 min
  • Dynamic Stretch – 2–3 min
  • Light Jog or Spot Run – 2 min

⚡ 2. Advanced HIIT Circuit

⏱ 40 sec ON / 20 sec OFF

  • Burpees
  • High Knees
  • Jump Squats
  • Plank Jacks
  • Mountain Climbers
  • Tuck Jumps

🔥 Total: ~20–25 mins

💪 3. Core Sculpt Circuit (ABS + OBLIQUES)

👉 3 rounds

  • Hanging Leg Raises / Lying Leg Raises – 15 reps
  • Bicycle Crunch – 20 reps
  • Russian Twists (weighted if possible) – 20 reps
  • Plank Hold – 60 sec
  • Heel Touches – 20 reps

🔥 4. Belly Fat Finisher

👉 2–3 rounds

  • Sprint in place – 30 sec
  • Slow crunch – 15 reps
  • Flutter kicks – 30 sec
  • Rest – 30 sec

🧘 5. Cool Down

  • Lower back stretch
  • Core stretch
  • Deep breathing

Thursday (Full Body Fat Burn + Conditioning)

🔥 1. Warm-Up (same base, slightly active)

  • Light Jog / Cycle – 5 min
  • Dynamic Stretch – 3 min
  • Bodyweight Squats – 15
  • Arm Swings + Shoulder Mobility

🔄 2. Full Body Metabolic Circuit

👉 4 rounds (continuous movement)

  • Dumbbell Thrusters (light/moderate) – 15 reps
  • Push-Ups – 15 reps
  • Dumbbell Deadlift (light) – 15 reps
  • Renegade Rows – 10 each side
  • Alternating Reverse Lunges – 12 each leg

⏱ Rest: 30–40 sec

⚡ 3. Functional Burn Circuit

👉 3 rounds

  • Kettlebell Swings – 15 reps
  • Box Step-Ups / Jump Step-Ups – 12 each leg
  • Battle Rope / Fast Punches – 30 sec
  • Bear Crawl – 30 sec

🔥 4. Cardio Finisher

👉 Choose 1:

Option A (Best)

  • Incline Treadmill Walk
  • 12–15 min
  • Incline 8–10

Option B (HIIT)

  • Rowing / Cycling
  • 20 sec fast + 40 sec slow × 10 rounds

🧘 5. Cool Down

  • Full body stretch
  • Focus: shoulders, legs, back

Friday (Explosive Fat Burn + Athletic Conditioning)

🔥 1. Warm-Up

  • High Knees – 2 min
  • Butt Kicks – 2 min
  • Jump Rope – 2 min
  • Dynamic Stretch – 2–3 min

⚡ 2. Explosive Power Circuit

👉 4 rounds

  • Jump Squats – 15 reps
  • Plyometric Push-Ups / Fast Push-Ups – 10–12 reps
  • Lateral Bounds – 15 each side
  • Broad Jumps (forward jumps) – 10 reps
  • Skater Jumps – 20 reps

⏱ Rest: 40 sec

🔄 3. Agility + Conditioning Circuit

👉 3–4 rounds

  • Shuttle Runs (forward-backward) – 30 sec
  • Ladder Drill / Quick Feet – 30 sec
  • Cone Drills (zig-zag run) – 30 sec
  • Bear Crawl – 30 sec

🔥 4. Killer Fat-Loss Finisher

👉 2–3 rounds

  • Burpees – 12 reps
  • Mountain Climbers – 40 sec
  • Sprint – 30 sec
  • Rest – 30 sec

🧘 5. Cool Down

  • Legs + shoulders stretch
  • Deep breathing

Saturday (CrossFit Fat Burn + Conditioning)

🔥 1. Warm-Up

  • Rowing / Cycling – 5 min
  • Dynamic Stretch – 3 min
  • Air Squats – 15
  • Push-ups – 10

⚡ 2. WOD (Workout of the Day)

👉 AMRAP 18 (As Many Rounds As Possible in 18 min)

  • 10 Push-Ups
  • 15 Air Squats
  • 10 Dumbbell Rows (each arm)
  • 12 Walking Lunges
  • 8 Burpees

🔄 3. Strength + Conditioning Block (Short & Effective)

👉 3 rounds

  • Kettlebell Swings – 15 reps
  • Goblet Squats (light/moderate) – 12 reps
  • Plank – 45 sec
  • Farmer’s Walk – 30 sec

🔥 4. Fat Loss Finisher

👉 2–3 rounds

  • Sprint / Fast Cycle – 30 sec
  • Rest – 30 sec
  • Jump Rope – 60 sec
  • Mountain Climbers – 40 sec

🧘 5. Cool Down

  • Full body stretching
  • Focus on legs + shoulders + lower back

🎯 Why These Alternates Are Better for You

  • Designed for fat loss + lean muscle (not bulk)
  • Use moderate weights + higher reps
  • Focus on continuous movement (less rest)
  • Include cardio finishers (key for fat loss)
  • Add functional + explosive movements for better shaping

👉 Result:

  • Leaner body
  • Better muscle definition
  • Faster fat loss
  • No bulky look

💡 Final Important Tips (Game-Changers)

Weight Selection

  • ❌ Don’t lift maximum heavy
  • ✅ Use moderate weight + control

Rest Time

  • Keep rest 30–45 seconds 👉 This keeps fat-burning active

Cardio Finishers (Must Do)

  • Minimum 10–15 mins after workout 👉 This is where real fat loss happens

Intensity Rule

  • Workout at 70–85% effort 👉 You should feel slightly breathless, not exhausted

Diet Matters More (70% Result)

  • High protein
  • Controlled carbs
  • No random junk at night

Consistency > Perfection

  • Follow plan 5–6 days/week
  • Rotation + discipline = visible results

🔥 Final Insight

You don’t need more workouts—you need smarter workouts. This rotation system ensures: ✔ No plateau ✔ Continuous fat loss ✔ Lean, toned physique
